## Fusing

Fusion is a big part of Endless and many builds need it. Fusing means using the Master Ball tier item, the **DNA Splicer**, to combine two Pokémon. Splicing averages their stats, changes their typing, combines their movepools, and more.

## Fuse tutoring

Fuse tutoring is one of the most important parts of PokeRogue and a foundation of Endless strategy. It gives Pokémon moves and abilities they can't learn by level-up or TM, using the DNA Splicer.

When you use a splicer you pick two party members, and **the order matters a lot**. A fused Pokémon keeps the **passive of the first** and the **ability of the second**. Unfusing gives you back the first Pokémon; the second is *gone forever*. What you keep is the first Pokémon with its updated moveset.

That's why starters with rare moves (like Dark Void and Mat Block) and useful passives are so valuable: fuse tutoring can put any move or ability on a Pokémon, so bringing Pokémon with common moves like Leech Seed is a waste.

How moves carry over:

- The fusion has the first Pokémon's moves and tries to learn the moves the second Pokémon had. Boosted PP from PP Ups or PP Max is lost.
- While fused, it can learn everything the second Pokémon learns by TM and level-up. Even if the second was a starter, it **can't** recall that Pokémon's egg moves.
- After unfusing, it can't relearn anything from the Pokémon it was fused with, but it **keeps** moves it learned while fused. Forget one and it's gone.

## Fuse feeding

Fuse feeding means fusing and unfusing a wild Pokémon that holds vitamins, to move those vitamins onto your Pokémon fast. Put the Pokémon you want the vitamins on **first** and the one carrying them **second**. Much faster than finding vitamins in the shop one by one.

## Spliced Endless

**How do I unlock it?** Beat Classic with a spliced Pokémon. The DNA Splicer is a Master Ball tier item in every mode except Spliced Endless, where it is Great Ball tier.

How it differs from normal Endless:

- Every Pokémon is spliced, wild ones and yours.
- DNA Splicers are Great Ball tier.
- Unspliced Pokémon have their base stat total cut in half.
- Best for speedrunning.
- More freedom in team building.
- It can be hard to find a good fusion for your carry.
- Wild fusions make run enders and failed shinies more common.
- Individual Pokémon gain less friendship.
- Higher skill requirement: more things to keep track of.
