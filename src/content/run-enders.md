## Neutralizing Gas

![Weezing](sprite:weezing-galar)

Believe it or not, **Weezing** is probably the biggest run ender in the game. Its ability Neutralizing Gas turns off **all** abilities, including Run Away and Sturdy, and at high waves you only do 1 damage because of tokens. Garbodor, Skuntank, Musharna and Volcanion can have it too.

Endless players have an answer: **Mat Block on the waiter + Whirlwind on the runner.**

![Runner with Whirlwind and waiter with Mat Block, ready for Neutralizing Gas](shots/neut-gas-runner.png)

![The waiter: Dark Void, Mat Block, Powder Snow](shots/neut-gas-waiter.png)

You can also use **Dark Void + Gastro Acid** to turn the ability off. In a single battle, you can just throw a Master Ball.

Not everyone has Dark Void unlocked or a spare slot, and Dark Void needs speed investment. **Fake Out + Roar** is a minimum-prep answer, but it won't always work, so get a proper one as soon as you can.

## Paradox bosses

The Paradox and Eternatus bosses to watch out for, and how to counter them.

### ![Flutter Mane](sprite:flutter-mane) Flutter Mane

- **Perish Song** makes everything that hears it faint in 3 turns, but bosses are immune, so only your Pokémon faint.
- **Dazzling** blocks priority moves, a problem for Prankster.
- It can have **Mean Look**, which traps you.
- *Counter for Perish Song:* switch into a sac so you can send the boss killer back in. That's why both boss-killing Pokémon have a pivot: Explosion / Teleport on the support, and Curse effectively pivots the boss killer.

### ![Scream Tail](sprite:scream-tail) Scream Tail

- Can have **Perish Song**. Same counter.

### ![Iron Thorns](sprite:iron-thorns) Iron Thorns

- **Sandstorm** chip damage happens before any healing, killing Shedinjas and Sturdy Pokémon that aren't Rock, Ground or Steel. *Counter:* some form of weather control, or a Rock / Ground / Steel boss killer.
- **Pin Missile** is multi-hit and breaks Sturdy. *Counter:* Disable / Torment plus a Reviver Seed on your runner.
- **Stealth Rock.** *Counter:* Defog / Tidy Up clear it, but Protect on the boss killer and support is better: heal the damage off with Protect before continuing.

### ![Gouging Fire](sprite:gouging-fire) Gouging Fire

- **Burning Bulwark** is a Protect that burns on contact, killing Shedinja. *Counter:* bait it, then attack when it's likely to fail.

### ![Iron Hands](sprite:iron-hands) Iron Hands

- **Focus Punch** has lower priority than Metal Burst, which breaks Metal Burst strategies. *Counter:* Counter, which has even lower priority.

### ![Slither Wing](sprite:slither-wing) Slither Wing

- **Scrappy** gets through Normalise and Normal/Ghost or Dark/Ghost Shedinja. *Counter:* Gastro Acid.
- **Dual Wingbeat** hits twice and breaks Sturdy. *Counter:* Disable / Torment.
- **Morning Sun** heals. *Counter:* Heal Block.

### ![Brute Bonnet](sprite:brute-bonnet) Brute Bonnet

- **Ingrain** heals more. *Counter:* Heal Block.
- **Dark type**, so Prankster fails, and **Grass type**, so Leech Seed fails. *Counter:* Soak. If your Prankster is the one with Soak: don't.
- Can have **Sucker Punch**. *Counter:* Psychic Terrain.

### ![Iron Crown](sprite:iron-crown) Iron Crown

- **Tachyon Cutter** hits twice and breaks Sturdy. *Counter:* Disable / Torment.

### ![Iron Boulder](sprite:iron-boulder) Iron Boulder

- **Mighty Cleave** goes through Protect and similar moves. *Counter:* Disable / Torment.

### ![Roaring Moon](sprite:roaring-moon) Roaring Moon

- **Dark type.** *Counter:* Soak, and don't use Prankster.

## Eternatus fusions

![Eternatus at wave 5750 against a Soak user](shots/eternatus-soak.png)

The dangerous Eternatus fusions:

- **![Reshiram](sprite:reshiram) Eteram (Reshiram).** Turboblaze (Mold Breaker) ignores all abilities. *Counter:* a speed-invested Gastro Acid, or a Reviver Seed.
- **![Zekrom](sprite:zekrom) Etem (Zekrom).** Teravolt (Mold Breaker) ignores all abilities. *Counter:* a speed-invested Gastro Acid, or a Reviver Seed.
- **![Calyrex](sprite:calyrex) Eternex (Calyrex).** **Unnerve** stops you eating berries; *counter:* Gastro Acid. It's **Grass** type; *counter:* Soak.
- **![Miraidon](sprite:miraidon) Eteidon (Miraidon).** Sets **Electric Terrain**, which prevents sleep. That shouldn't matter, since you shouldn't be putting Paradoxes and Eternatus to sleep. *Counter:* Psychic Terrain.
- **![Zygarde](sprite:zygarde-50) Eterde (Zygarde).** **Power Construct** can't be overwritten by Entrainment or Skill Swap. Don't get into that situation. *Counter:* use your boss killer like you're supposed to.

## Shiny obstacles

**Abilities that stop phazing.** If a shiny's partner is **Gholdengo**, you can't easily Roar or Whirlwind it away: **Good as Gold** stops every status move, so Gastro Acid, Roar and Whirlwind all fail. Use Gastro Acid from a **Mold Breaker** Pokémon, or **Core Enforcer**, which disables Good as Gold.

**Magic Bounce** is another reason for Mold Breaker Gastro Acid: normal Gastro Acid won't work. Dragon Tail or Scrappy Circle Throw are alternatives, but Dragon Tail fails on Hatterene and Circle Throw fails on Gholdengo. **Hatterene** is a Fairy type with Magic Bounce, so Core Enforcer doesn't work on it either. That's why a Mold Breaker Gastro Acid Pokémon is good for this rare case; otherwise Core Enforcer is better, because it's a spread move that disables both abilities and passives. A hidden-ability Hatterene is **extremely** rare.

**Magic Guard** is a problem on X0 waves, where you can't phaze: it blocks all indirect damage, so DoT does nothing. Gastro Acid or Core Enforcer removes it. Rarely it's fused with Good as Gold or Magic Bounce, so you need Mold Breaker Gastro Acid or Core Enforcer. Outside X0 waves, Mold Breaker Roar removes these Pokémon immediately, but Mold Breaker Gastro Acid is still worth having for other abilities.

**Comatose** keeps the user permanently asleep, yet it can still attack, and the ability can't be replaced, suppressed or ignored. The only answer is luck with flinches, or Quash. Luckily only wild Komala, Quagsire and Slaking get it, and Jirachi can't be found in the wild right now.

## Wild Pokémon killing their partner

Spread moves are a problem: Damage tokens outgrow Protection tokens, so late in Endless a wild Pokémon that uses a move hitting the whole field kills its own partner, unless that partner has Sturdy or an Endure token procs, and you can't rely on either.

The answer is **Dark Void** from a speed-invested Pokémon, which puts both to sleep. If Electric Terrain is up, switch in a Psychic Surge Pokémon to override it, or use Ice Spinner / Steel Roller. Rock Slide with Multi Lens (hoping for flinches) or Powder Snow (freezes, and with 3 King's Rocks it flinches as often as Rock Slide) also work.

Normalise Entrainment builds have their own way: Trick-or-Treat on the shiny and Entrainment to give the other Pokémon Normalise, so it can't damage anything. Or use Entrainment to give the shiny **Telepathy**, so its partner can't damage it.

## Pokémon that faint themselves

Pokémon with self-fainting moves are annoying when one keeps blowing up next to a new shiny. Usually you put them to sleep, but **Minior** is immune to status above half HP, **Hatterene's** Magic Bounce bounces any status move, and **Garganacl's** Purifying Salt blocks all status.
