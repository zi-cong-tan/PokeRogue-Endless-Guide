## Before the run

This chapter walks through a run from team building to the end. Read [Forming an Endless team](#forming-an-endless-team) first.

The most important decision comes before the run starts: **which starters to bring**. Starters are the **only** way to get **passives** in Endless; you can't get them mid-run, so every Pokémon you bring wants its passive. Not all passives are equally useful. If you want an easier run, or more shinies, choose them carefully (the team chapter lists the passives for each role). If you have the Pokémon but not the passive, farm it in Classic: you get 3x more candy there than in Endless.

## Building the team

![The two halves of a build: waiter and runner fused with lures](shots/build-half-waiter-runner.png)

**Waves 1000 to 2000** are normally where you run, but if you haven't got everything yet, that's fine. This is where you build: once your build is set up you can start running right away. You don't need to wait for wave 2000; the earlier the better.

Here you collect your moves and items. With your first [DNA Splicer](#fusing), [fuse](#fuse-tutoring) your [tutor](#tutor) onto your [waiter](#waiter) to get Mat Block and/or Dark Void. Then use [Sandstorm's PokeRogue Dex](https://sandstormer.github.io/PokeRogue-Dex/) to find what you still need.

![Searching the PokeRogue SearchDex for Pokémon that learn the moves you need](shots/searchdex.png)

Add the biome you're in at the end of the search with "and", so you know which Pokémon to look out for. You'll also collect your items here: Shiny, Ability and Catching Charms, Lock Capsule, Master Balls, Amulet Coins, Berry Pouch and so on.

Once the [runner](#runner) and the waiter have all their moves, fuse them with lure abilities. The one fused onto your waiter must be different from its passive, and one of the two must be No Guard (not for the [post berry rework](#post-berry-rework-serene-grace) builds, which avoid No Guard). That's half your build.

![The other half: boss killer and support with Sturdy](shots/build-half-boss-killer-support.png)

The other two are the [boss killer](#boss-killer) and the [support](#sturdy-support). Build them in any order, as you find things. Once they have their moves, find a wild Pokémon with Sturdy and fuse it onto them.

When all four have the right moves, abilities and items, you're ready to run. If you're unsure how it works, keep using your carry for a while and practise your boss-killing method (here, [DoT](#damage-over-time)) on the Paradoxes. Once you're confident, start running.

## Running

Once your build is set up and you have at least a few **Master Balls**, run. This is where you get shinies fastest, because you see more Pokémon per minute.

Each time you use a Master Ball, reroll the shop until you get another. Sometimes you'll catch a shiny on an X0 wave or can't find another Master Ball, which is why you want a few. Running low? Catch non-bosses with Rogue Balls to reach the shop and buy more. Shinies have a 2x catch bonus, so Rogue Balls catch them very reliably.

## Catching shinies

On your waiter use **Mat Block** or **Dark Void**; on your runner use a **phazing** move (Roar, Dragon Tail…) to remove the non-shiny Pokémon. Then throw a ball.

## X0 waves

On X0 waves you can't phaze, so it's harder. You have to [DoT](#damage-over-time) the non-shiny down.

**Before the berry rework (now).** Mat Block with the waiter and switch your DoT Pokémon in for the runner. Dark Void with the waiter and DoT the non-shiny. Repeat until all three DoTs are on, or both enemies are out of Lum Berries and asleep. From then on the waiter relies on RNG with Powder Snow. With the DoTs on, switch the boss killer for the support and use Heal Block, Soak and so on until the non-shiny faints. With a bit of luck your waiter is still alive in the first slot: throw a Master Ball, and the next wave is still a double battle. If your runner isn't on the field next wave, Mat Block with the waiter and switch the runner in safely to keep running.

**After the berry rework (1.13).** Mat Block with the waiter and switch the runner out for your Prankster support. The waiter uses Muddy Water or Rock Slide while the Prankster uses Entrainment / Skill Swap on the waiter to give it Serene Grace. Then use Rock Slide and Muddy Water to flinch the wild Pokémon; if one doesn't flinch on a turn, reload and use the other move. Meanwhile, switch the other slot to the boss killer and apply the DoTs, then to the Sturdy support for Soak and Heal Block (before the DoTs if the non-shiny is Grass type). When the non-shiny faints, throw a Master Ball at the shiny. If your runner isn't on the field next wave, Mat Block and switch it in.
